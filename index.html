Transparent model

Each trial draws correlated home/away scores from a bivariate normal centered on expected points derived from offensive, defensive, and recent-form ratings, plus home-field advantage. Scores are rounded to integers and floored at zero.

score_away = E[away] + σ · Z₁ score_home = E[home] + σ · (ρ·Z₁ + √(1−ρ²)·Z₂) Z₁, Z₂ ~ iid N(0,1) → Corr ≈ ρ
