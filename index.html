Transparent model

Expected points are market-implied from the median consensus home spread and total across DraftKings (via ESPN), FanDuel, BetMGM, and Bovada when available. Noise is a bivariate normal around those means; scores are rounded to integers and floored at zero. ATS cover rates sit near 50% by design; win probability is driven by the spread.

marginExp = −spread homeExp = (total − spread) / 2 awayExp = (total + spread) / 2 score_away = E[away] + σ · Z₁ score_home = E[home] + σ · (ρ·Z₁ + √(1−ρ²)·Z₂) Z₁, Z₂ ~ iid N(0,1) → Corr ≈ ρ
